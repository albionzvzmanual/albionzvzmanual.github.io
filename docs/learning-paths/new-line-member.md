# Your first CTA

<p class="lead">Outcome: explain your job and recognize when you cannot safely follow a call.</p><p>A CTA is a scheduled group activity. ZvZ refers to large-group warfare. The first task is to remain useful to the group while learning one role.</p>

## Prepare

<p>Confirm the assigned role, meeting point and communication channel. Check the selected abilities and controls before entering danger. Ask which call means stop or reset.</p>

## Follow the intent

<p>“Move south” may reposition the group for a turn. Follow the route while keeping access to the allies your role supports. Report an obstacle or inability to follow instead of silently substituting another plan.</p>

## Read connection

<p>Before advancing, identify where help can reach you and where you could move next. Being alive but unable to contribute is different from successfully supporting the group.</p>

## Review one decision

<p>After the fight, find a moment when you lost useful position. Rewind to the decision that caused it and choose one change for the next attempt.</p><details><summary>Readiness check: the caller turns, but you are separated by terrain. What do you do?</summary><p>Report the separation briefly, take the agreed safe route if available, and avoid forcing an isolated entry just to match the direction. The useful question is whether your role can contribute to the call, not whether you clicked the same compass direction.</p></details>
