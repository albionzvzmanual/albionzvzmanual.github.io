# Current scope

Version 4 organizes the manual around learning, role decisions, practice and reference lookup. Start with the beginner path, choose a role, practise a decision or look up a mechanic. The historical lesson addresses continue to work.

The patch chronology and verification date for an individual mechanic can differ. A dated change establishes what happened in that release. Read the evidence beside the lesson before relying on a specific value. [Current-value checks](../weapon-notes/patch-sensitive-notes.md).

Historical examples illustrate how a group made a decision under particular conditions. Select a role, weapon or formation according to your own group’s assignment. The [Formation Ledger](../workbooks/index.md) is an optional companion.
