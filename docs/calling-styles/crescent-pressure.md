# Crescent Pressure

Some groups use this name for pressure along a shallow arc. Its value comes from which routes the group can cover together.

## Read the situation

The outside of the arc can see a retreat route. Extending farther would put it around a corner from its healer. Is covering that route worth the separation?

??? question "Compare your call"

    A shallow angle can still deny a route while keeping support in reach. If the enemy turns outward, shorten the arc or shift support before holding the exposed end. A wider shape alone gives no advantage.
