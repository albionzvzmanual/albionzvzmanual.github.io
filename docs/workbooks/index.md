## What the ledger is for

Use the Ledger when your group wants one place to track:

- signups
- role assignments
- party structure
- build references
- coverage checks
- scaling order
- comp package context
- common mistakes and notes

## What the ledger is not

The Ledger is not a meta list and not a replacement for the manual.

The current public sheet includes **Great Fire Control Formation** as a filled example package. It demonstrates how a comp package can be documented. It does not claim that every group should play that formation.

## How to use it safely

1. Learn the general doctrine in the manual.
2. Make a copy of the Ledger.
3. Document your own roster and comp assumptions.
4. Use the sheet to find coverage gaps.
5. Review whether your comp's execution burden matches your roster.
