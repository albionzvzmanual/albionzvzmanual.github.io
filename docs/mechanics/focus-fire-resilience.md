# Focus Fire and Resilience

Focus fire and resilience mechanics help explain why blindly throwing the whole zerg into one protected target can be low value.

The lesson is not "never focus a target." The lesson is: focus the target that can actually die or create the next useful fight window.

## Season 34 check: protection and penetration are different

Dragonfire set Resilience Penetration to zero as a seasonal test, and the Season 34 announcement confirmed its removal for that season. This is a dated change to penetration, not a statement that Focus Fire Protection itself was removed. Historical melee-versus-ranged explanations that rely on positive penetration values must be rechecked.

For current decisions, assess the selected kit, target, support, and timing rather than carry forward an old penetration advantage. The seasonal test does not establish a permanent weapon ranking. Reviewed 25 October 2026; the reviewed subsequent live patch notes contain no reversal. [Dragonfire changelog](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1842212951309746); [Season 34 announcement](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1842846814442809).

## Field behavior

- Pressure front targets with Q/W, but do not waste the whole engage on an isolated protected tank unless there is a reason.
- Look for clumps, low targets, exposed healers, enemy DPS channels, and valuable support cooldowns.
- Scoreboard damage is not the same as fight impact.
- Utility, pierce, heal cut, and terrain can make a target worth focusing when raw damage alone would not.

## Practical rule

Ask what the focus creates: a kill, a cooldown trade, a forced retreat, a terrain win, or only wasted damage.
