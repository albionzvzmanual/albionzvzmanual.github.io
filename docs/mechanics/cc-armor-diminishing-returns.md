# Crowd control, armor and diminishing returns

An enemy tank is already through a gate. Two damage dealers are still approaching it. Before using a stop, identify the action that threatens your party. Preventing those players from reaching a firing position may buy more recovery time than stunning the tank.

## Match the effect to the action

| What you need | What to check before committing |
| --- | --- |
| Keep an enemy out of a lane | A root restricts movement. An enemy already in range may still cast from that position. |
| Prevent a spell | A silence can deny spell use while the target can still move. For a channel already underway, check its interruptibility and your tool's effect. |
| Hold a hittable target | A stun can prevent action during its effective duration. Allies need sight, reach and time to use that window. |
| Move an enemy | A displacement changes the target's position. Check where it will land relative to allied damage and the route you need to protect. |

## Read three different defenses

**Armor and magic resistance** mitigate physical and magical damage respectively. A damage-resistance reduction can increase follow-up damage while leaving the target free to act. Identify the relevant stat on the selected ability.

**Crowd control resistance** can shorten applicable control effects. Check the particular effect. For example, Radiant Wilds Patch 3 removed the dependence of Holy Explosion’s knockback distance on CC duration and the enemy’s CC resistance. Using another spell's duration to predict this displacement would give a misleading answer.

**Diminishing returns** can reduce the effective duration of successive control applied to a target. Two nominal durations added together can overstate the usable window even if the casts do not overlap. A cleanse can also change the state of the chain. Rogue Frontier introduced a partial reset of diminishing returns for the effect removed. Keep the effect type, any cleanse, and the timing of reapplication in your notes.

**Immunity, cleanse and purge** answer different questions. Immunity can prevent an applicable effect from taking hold. A cleanse can remove an applicable effect already on a target. A purge can remove a removable benefit, potentially making a later control effect possible. Check the selected ability and the target state before assuming any of these will work.

## Work through the gate

You can root the first damage dealer just before the gate, or after they cross. If the enemy still lacks a firing angle, the earlier root can keep your healer out of reach. If they already have the angle, rooting them may leave the dangerous cast available. A teammate with an appropriate interrupt may have the better answer. Check the second damage dealer before both of you spend a major stop on the first.

??? question "How would you diagnose a short stop in a replay?"

    Record the effect that landed, prior control of that type, visible cleanses or immunities, and when the target next performed the relevant action. Separate an early cleanse from a short duration and a missed cast. If the replay cannot distinguish them, repeat with one target and one changed condition.
