# Bomb / Clap Concepts

Bomb/clap style tries to kill or heavily punish enemies inside a short window.

## What to watch

- enemy mobility
- clump tanks
- stacked DPS
- defensive cooldown bait
- where damage will land
- whether they can reset after the bomb

## Common response

After the first hit, identify which group spent its tools. Check for a second damage wave and confirm that your recovery route still works. A reachable counter is one option. Hold a protected angle if the reserve remains unseen.

## Not a full comp guide

This page explains the style concept only. It does not provide a full bomb comp.
