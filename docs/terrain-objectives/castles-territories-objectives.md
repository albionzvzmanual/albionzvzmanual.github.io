# Castles, Territories, and Objective Geometry

## Core rule

Objective fights are still ZvZ fights.

Objectives change the terrain, timing, and risk profile. They do not remove the need for spacing, support, healing, tanks, role discipline, and cooldown reads.

## Identify the objective and mode first

| Objective context | Decision to establish before moving |
|---|---|
| Outlands castle | Which approach, entry, and exit can the group contest for this castle objective? |
| Outlands territory | Which territory structure or objective matters now, and what access or contest rules apply? |
| Faction Warfare Battle Standard | Who is carrying the moving objective, and can the escort reach an eligible delivery point? |
| Faction Warfare Bandit Assault | Is the group contesting Supplies or a later Fortress fight, and which route preserves that objective? |

Faction Fortresses, Outlands castles, and Outlands territories are distinct systems. Similar doors and choke points do not imply identical capture rules, schedules, repairs, rewards, or targeting behavior.

Realm Divided Part II introduced carriable Faction Battle Standards and reworked Bandit Assault around Supplies and later Fortress contests. A Standard can make an escort route more important than chasing kills; a Fortress contest can concentrate pressure on access and exits. These are tactical implications of the objectives, not additional capture rules. [Official release, 23 February 2026](https://albiononline.com/news/realm-divided-part-ii-live).

For a Faction Battle Standard escort, do not plan a refuge around Smuggler Stash invulnerability: Radiant Wilds Patch 4 removed that protection for Standard carriers. Preserve a defensible route and exit instead. [Final live changelog, 21 July 2026](https://albiononline.com/changelog/radiant-wilds-patch-4).

Before committing, check the live map and objective UI for the relevant mode, eligibility, stage, and destination. Do not import an Outlands siege checklist into a Faction fight unchanged.

## Dragonfire relevance to large-scale fighting

Open-world Dragon encounters can attract rival groups, particularly in the Outlands. The PvP lesson is to assign scouts, preserve an exit, and decide who watches for other players while the group is occupied. This is an inference about contested open-world activity, not a Dragon boss guide. [Dragonfire overview](https://albiononline.com/news/dragonfire-coming).

Ancient Lands use their own portal and encounter rules. Treat any decision to enter as a separate activity with its own group and loss conditions, not as an extension of an Outlands territory fight. Portal adjustments alone do not justify a new ZvZ curriculum chapter. Dragon Sanctuary boss strategies, loot optimization, and cosmetic rewards are outside this manual's purpose. [Development update, 16 October 2026](https://albiononline.com/news/dev-update-october-2026).

## Objective geometry

Castles, territories, towers, mages, doors, gates, walls, repair positions, and chest rooms create special geometry:

- some walls are strong attack points
- some walls are traps
- doors can split formations
- corners behave like chokes
- over-wall pressure changes safety
- repair position matters
- objective pressure forces movement even when kills are not happening

## Walls and gates are not equal

A wall or gate may be bad if:

- attackers can be bombed over terrain
- retreat path is exposed
- repairer or channeler is unsafe
- counter-engage path is poor
- DPS cannot follow the clump
- the enemy has a better corner or angle

## Tanking inside vs outside

Sometimes tanks must play inside a broken wall or gate to stop enemy bombs before they reach DPS/healers. Sometimes that is too advanced or too risky for the roster.

The correct answer depends on:

- healer reach
- enemy bomb path
- support reliability
- terrain shape
- roster maturity
- objective value

## Objective pressure

Q/W pressure on reachable enemy players can force defensives and movement without spending full engage cooldowns. Pressure near a door or wall can change access, but do not assume a player-damage ability can damage a structure; check that objective's rules and required tools.

Do not treat objective fights as pure clump-and-dump. Pressure and position often decide whether the enemy gets to play.

## Patch-sensitive warning

Exact objective mechanics, guard behavior, mage damage, repair rules, wall behavior, and over-wall targeting can change. Public doctrine should teach the decision logic first and verify exact mechanics when needed.

## Recheck objective value as seasons change

Radiant Wilds Patch 3 changed territory point distribution, control costs and penalties, raid energy destruction, and hideout durability. These affect what is worth contesting and how much time or pressure a plan requires. The general lessons about routes and connected support remain useful, but older economic or siege-duration assumptions should not drive a current call. Check the applicable season and structure rules before setting priorities. [Publisher changelog, 29 June 2026](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1836506165559763).

Keeper Uprising objectives belonged to the intervening seasonal event. Do not teach that temporary event as an evergreen objective schedule.

## Training prompt

For an objective fight, ask:

- What terrain feature decided the call?
- Which wall/gate/door was actually valuable?
- Did tanks stand where objective pressure mattered?
- Did safe players create pressure or just wait?
- Did the zerg clump in a "safe" corner that became a trap?
