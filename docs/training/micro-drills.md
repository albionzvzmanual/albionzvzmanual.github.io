# Micro-Drills

## Kite route discipline

Caller announces a route. Players pause the VOD and mark where their role should be five seconds later.

## Turn-window readiness

Pause after enemy engage lands. Identify who is safe and who should walk forward.

## Q/W pressure floor

Choose one player and an assigned effect. During the gap between major hits, mark a cast that delayed pursuit, applied useful pressure or enabled recovery. Also mark one moment when holding the tool preserved a better response.

## Danger-area healing

Pause before impact. Name the endangered player, the healer assigned to reach them and where both can move next. Continue through recovery and inspect whether the chosen heal arrived while it could still change the result.

## Support-layer timing

Track whether Locus, Oathkeepers, Rootbound, Demon/Judi/Guard, and personal defensives are layered on danger or wasted on safe space.

## Plate/frontline discipline

Name the plate player’s assigned threat and ready tool. Mark the position where they could affect that threat while keeping a route back to support.
