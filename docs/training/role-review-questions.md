# Role-Specific VOD Questions

Use these questions to turn vague feedback into concrete review.

## All players

- Did I understand the call's intent?
- Was I connected to healers/supports?
- Did I create or avoid friendly clumps?
- Did I keep playing after enemy cooldowns were spent?
- Did I leave the fight while still healthy?

## DPS

- Did my damage land at zero?
- Did I use Q/W pressure after E?
- Did I take a real angle or hide in a backline clump?
- Did I hit a valuable target, or only the easiest one?
- Did I keep pressure on low enemies?

## Healers

- Did I heal danger or only low HP?
- Did I place healing where players were going?
- Did I walk so much that I stopped healing?
- Did I cover the crossing, tail, or forward pressure lane?
- Did my healing teach players to play correctly?

## Supports

- Did I cover the real danger lane?
- Did I use tools before impact or after deaths?
- Did I pair with other support layers when needed?
- Did I use Q/W/armor/boots, or only E?
- Did I leave after placing value or add AoE escalation?

## Defensive tanks

- Did I stop enemy DPS path or only enemy tanks?
- Did I cover flank/counter path before damage landed?
- Did I stay until the zerg was safe?
- Did I chase one low target while the formation died?
- Was I front enough when full HP?

## Engage / clump tanks

- Was my clump visible before zero?
- Could DPS hit it?
- Did I hold enemies inside allied damage?
- Did I create value or pull enemies away from damage?
- Did I have an exit or next step?

## Callers / officers

- Did the call match our roster and comp style?
- Did the call have a next step?
- Did scouts and kill feed support the decision?
- Did we read role casualties, not only total deaths?
- Did we ask the roster to execute something it was not ready for?
