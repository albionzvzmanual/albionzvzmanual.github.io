# Review 10–15 Seconds Before Death

## Core rule

The death frame is often the result, not the mistake.

Review the setup before the death.

## Why this matters

Players often say:

> "I died because their bomb hit me."

That may be true, but incomplete. The useful review is often:

- Why was I in that clump?
- Why was I on that wall?
- Why was I out of heal range?
- Why did I walk away from support?
- Why did I not use mobility earlier?
- Why was no tank covering that path?
- Why was I late to the call?

## Review method

For each important death, pause at:

1. 15 seconds before death
2. 10 seconds before death
3. 5 seconds before death
4. impact
5. 3 seconds after impact

Ask what changed at each point.

## What to look for

- wrong side of terrain
- slow mount-up or dismount
- following literal call instead of intent
- clumping with safe players
- leaving the active fight
- support tool placed too late or wrong lane
- healer out of danger range
- tank failing to stop enemy path
- DPS starting damage at zero instead of landing at zero

## Training prompt

Write one sentence:

> My death started when I ____.

If the sentence begins with "when their damage landed," go earlier.
