# New to ZvZ

ZvZ is not just a bigger small-scale fight. The important difference is that your individual movement affects the entire group's survival.

## Your first job

Your first job is not to top damage, make a hero play, or understand every call. Your first job is:

> Be in the correct place when the zerg needs you.

That means:

- avoid sharing the same enemy hit with nearby allies
- follow your assigned route and stay in reach of allies you need to help or receive help from
- turn only when you can do your job, allies are ready, and cover lets the group achieve something. A safe exit can be the whole plan
- learn one stable role before over-flexing
- use your defensive when it can answer the threat it is meant for
- review mistakes without turning critique into drama

## The first mistake most players make

New players often treat calls like literal movement commands.

If the caller says "south," they click south even if the actual goal is to build a front, protect a choke, or prepare a turn around terrain.

Better question:

> Where should my role be so this call works?

## First review habit

After your next CTA, do not ask "did I die?" only ask:

- Could I do my job from where I stood?
- Did we give the enemy an easy hit on several allies?
- After the enemy spent an attack, could we make a useful turn with cover, or did we need to finish leaving?
- Did I understand what my role was trying to do?

## Your first crossing

ZvZ means zerg versus zerg. A CTA is a call to arms or scheduled group activity. A caller coordinates the group, while scouts report movement and roles cover damage, healing, control or utility. On a crossing, look for enemies gathering toward the exit before damage arrives. Prepare your assigned response. A preventive shield and a cleanse aimed at an effect already applied need different cues.
