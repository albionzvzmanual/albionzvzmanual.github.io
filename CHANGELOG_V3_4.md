# Albion ZvZ Manual v3.4 Role Guide Expansion - Change Log

## Purpose

This update expands the Role Guides section into a stronger teaching layer for general ZvZ players. The role pages remain principle-based and non-meta-prescriptive.

## Major changes

- Expanded **All Players** into a stronger universal role-responsibility page.
- Expanded **DPS Overview** with damage-board caution, target logic, timing, Q/W pressure, and VOD prompts.
- Expanded **Ranged DPS** with functional range, angle discipline, cast timing, and post-bomb pressure.
- Expanded **Melee Pressure / Execute DPS** with role courage, first-engage vs counter-engage logic, chase discipline, and execute value.
- Expanded **Support DPS** with pierce/heal-cut timing and utility-window checks.
- Expanded **Healers**, **Holy Healers**, and **Nature Healers** with danger-priority, healing-zone discipline, movement, sustain, and VOD prompts.
- Expanded **Supports** with danger-lane ownership, invisible support value, preventive support, and support-stack completeness.
- Expanded **Defensive / Stopper Tanks** with front establishment, path denial, healable lines, and choke/crossing responsibilities.
- Expanded **Engage / Clump Tanks** with clump-before-zero, clump compatibility, and follow-up pressure.
- Expanded **Caller / Officers** with caller-style fit, tactical intent, secondary caller protocol, and VOD prompts.
- Expanded **Scouts / Info** with actionable reports, HVT watch, and threat-location-action callout habits.

## Public-safety decisions

- No weapon or comp is presented as the recommended meta.
- Tool examples are kept functional, not prescriptive.
- Private VOD material is generalized into reusable role principles.
- Exact current mechanics, item values, and meta claims remain outside the public role pages unless verified later.
