# Albion ZvZ Manual v3.4.1 Spacing and Mechanics Clarification - Change Log

## Purpose

This is a small content clarification patch after early public feedback. It keeps the manual's core values: general ZvZ education, non-meta-prescriptive framing, and clean public-safe wording.

## Changes

- Added **Focus Fire Protection / Resilience** to **Foundations > Spacing and AoE Escalation**.
- Clarified that AoE escalation explains why clumps die, while resilience helps explain why one player does not always die instantly just because many players hit them.
- Added **Spacing Depends on Intent** to **Fight Execution > Spread Does Not Mean Leave the Fight**.
- Avoided creating a rigid style-definition table, because Albion playstyle labels are often messy and can distract from the core lesson.
- Updated related links and overview wording where needed.

## Public-safety decisions

- No new meta, comp, or weapon recommendation was added.
- No private VOD examples were exposed.
- The spacing clarification is written around fight intent rather than contested labels like kite, brawl, control, bomb, or ranged.
- Exact numeric mechanics remain in external/current mechanics references rather than being hard-coded into the doctrine page.
